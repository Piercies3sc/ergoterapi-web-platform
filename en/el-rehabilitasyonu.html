Hand and Upper Extremity Rehabilitation

Regaining functionality, range of motion, and strength following injuries and surgical interventions of the hand, wrist, elbow, or shoulder.

Our Hands: Our Connection to the World

The human hand has an incredibly complex anatomy and flawless biomechanics. An injury to the hand or arm deeply disrupts daily life.

Occupational therapists specialized in hand rehabilitation work in close coordination with surgeons to ensure tissue healing. The goal is for the individual to actively use their hand in daily life again through special massage techniques, edema management, scar mobilization, strengthening exercises, and custom-made thermoplastic splints.

Applied Treatment Methods

Custom Splinting

Orthotic applications molded on the hand to protect healing tissues or increase range of motion.

Fine Motor Skill Development

Practicing precise movements such as grasping, pinching, and object manipulation.

Pain and Edema Management

Reducing swelling and relieving tissue sensitivity after trauma or surgery.

Scar Management

Preventing post-surgical tissue adhesions and applying scar mobilization techniques.

Healing Process

The steps we take to return function to your hand.

1

Acute Assessment

Evaluating tissue status, range of motion, and edema post-injury or surgery.

2

Protection and Early Movement

Fabricating necessary splints and beginning controlled early movements.

3

Strength and Endurance

Strengthening hand and arm muscles as healing progresses.

4

Functional Use

Full integration of the hand into daily life, profession, and hobbies.

Military Occupational Therapy

A specialized field supporting military personnel and veterans in returning to their active duties or daily civilian routines following physical or psychological trauma.

Independence on Duty and in Civilian Life

Military environments and operational tasks are areas where physical and mental boundaries are tested to their limits. Military occupational therapy plays a critical role in the rehabilitation of personnel who have experienced injuries, amputations, traumatic brain injuries (TBI), or post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).

The primary goal is not only physical recovery but also helping the individual regain their occupational identity, increasing independence through equipment adaptations, and maximizing their quality of life during the transition to civilian life.

Intervention and Support Areas

Cognitive Rehabilitation

Regaining memory, attention, and decision-making skills after a traumatic brain injury.

Physical Adaptation

Prosthesis training after amputation and environmental modifications for home or work.

Mental Health (PTSD)

Coping strategies for post-traumatic stress, anxiety, and sleep disorders.

Vocational Integration

Guiding veterans to civilian professions suited to their abilities and providing return-to-work support.

Rehabilitation Process

Our systematic and goal-oriented adaptation approach.

1

Comprehensive Assessment

Detailed analysis of physical, cognitive, and psychological state.

2

Goal Setting

Identifying the individual's independence goals in military or civilian life.

3

Intervention & Adaptation

Implementing prosthesis training, cognitive therapy, and environmental modifications.

4

Social Participation

Full independence in returning to work, social integration, and daily living activities.

Target Audience

We offer customized therapy programs for the following groups.

Active Duty Military Personnel Veterans (Combat and Disabled) Law Enforcement Officers Individuals with Duty-Related Trauma Individuals under Operational Stress
